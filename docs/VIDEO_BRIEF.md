# Quiloria — Hero Video Creative Brief

## Overview

We need a **10–15 second seamlessly looping 3D animation** for the hero section of [Quiloria](https://quiloria.app), a collaborative story-writing platform. The video sits in a gilded frame on a deep mahogany background, so it needs to feel warm, atmospheric, and magical — never cold or corporate.

The concept: **a split-screen showing a Writer and a Reader, connected by living words that transform into imagination.**

---

## Concept

### Left Side — The Writer
- A figure (stylized, slightly geometric/faceted — not photorealistic, not cartoonish) sits at a warm wooden desk
- They're writing with a quill or pen — the hand moves with purpose
- A single warm light source: a desk lantern or candle, casting golden volumetric light
- As they write, **luminous ink lifts off the page** — glowing gold particles/fluid that rises and drifts rightward
- The environment is cozy and intimate: dark background, warm wood tones, maybe a bookshelf faintly visible behind

### The Bridge — The Magic
- The luminous ink streams across the center divide — not in a straight line, but in an organic, flowing river of light
- The particles are warm gold (#C8963C) fading into softer amethyst (#7E5E9E) as they cross
- Mid-crossing, the ink begins to **coalesce and take form** — a shape emerges from the flow
- This is the key moment: formless ink becomes a majestic dragon, its wings beginning to unfurl
- The transformation should feel like calligraphy ink becoming light becoming life — fluid, organic, inevitable

### Right Side — The Reader
- A figure reclines in a chair or on a couch, holding an open book
- The book **glows from within** — the dragon emerges upward from its pages
- The dragon (now fully formed, wings unfurling) rises above the reader in luminous detail, casting golden and amethyst light across their face
- The reader tilts their head up slightly in wonder
- The environment mirrors the left but is slightly different — maybe a reading nook, a blanket, a floor lamp
- After a beat, the dragon begins to dissolve back into particles that drift leftward and downward — completing the loop

### The Loop Point
- The particles from the dissolving dragon drift back toward the writer's side
- They settle back onto the writer's page as fresh ink
- Seamless restart — the writer continues, new particles rise

## Visual Style

### Reference Board

| Reference | What to Take From It |
|---|---|
| *Monument Valley* (Ustwo Games) | Color palette, geometric stylization, sense of quiet wonder |
| *The Secret of Kells* (animated film) | The way ink becomes magic — ornamental, alive, intentional |
| *Ori and the Blind Forest* | Warm light particles, atmospheric depth, emotional glow |
| *Firewatch* (Campo Santo) | Stylized human figures — enough detail to feel real, abstract enough to be universal |
| Apple "Behind the Mac" ads | Split-screen storytelling rhythm, intimate creative moments |
| Ink-in-water macro photography | The fluid transformation from ink to form — organic, unpredictable, beautiful |

### Color Palette

Use these exact tones to match the website:

| Role | Hex | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Background/shadows | `#110E0A` | Deep mahogany, near-black but warm |
| Secondary dark | `#1A1510` | Dark wood tone |
| Warm mid-tone | `#2E2720` | Walnut shadow |
| **Primary accent** | `#C8963C` | Firelight gold — the ink, the glow, the magic |
| Gold highlight | `#E0B260` | Lighter gold for bright moments |
| Amethyst accent | `#7E5E9E` | Secondary magic color, reader's side |
| Teal accent | `#3B6E7A` | Subtle tertiary, dragon scale highlights |
| Text/skin highlight | `#F2E8D0` | Warm parchment — lightest tone |
| Ruby accent | `#9E3B42` | Warm red for subtle details (book spine, dragon eye) |

**Important:** No pure blacks (#000), no pure whites (#FFF), no cool blues or grays. Everything warm.

### Lighting
- **Volumetric and soft.** Think candlelight, not studio lights
- Writer's side: single warm point light (lantern/candle), casting soft shadows
- Reader's side: the book itself is a light source — cool-warm amethyst glow
- The particles/ink emit their own soft light as they travel
- Shallow depth of field / tilt-shift effect — edges go soft, center stays sharp
- Subtle dust motes floating in the light (very small, very slow)

### Geometry & Characters
- **Stylized low-poly to mid-poly** — faceted but smooth where it matters (faces, hands)
- Characters should feel universal — no specific age/race/gender. Silhouette-readable
- Environments are cozy and detailed enough to feel lived-in but not cluttered
- The dragon should be majestic and awe-inspiring but warm — not menacing or aggressive, more ancient and wise

### Texture
- Subtle paper/grain texture over the entire render (we add additional grain on the website, but bake some in)
- Wood surfaces should feel hand-carved, not photoscanned
- The book pages should glow like backlit parchment

## Placement Context

The video sits inside a gilded frame on the landing page:
- Deep mahogany (`#110E0A`) page background
- Gold corner ornaments on the frame
- Paper grain texture overlay (very subtle)
- Inner shadow for depth
- A play/pause button appears on hover (gold icon on dark backdrop)
- Below the video: italic caption "From the writer's imagination to the reader's world — stories that come alive."

The video auto-plays muted on scroll-into-view. It should look beautiful both playing and paused on any frame.

---

## What to Avoid
- Corporate/SaaS aesthetic — no flat illustrations, no isometric office scenes
- Cool tones — no blues, grays, or white backgrounds
- Photorealism — this is a storybook world, not a photograph
- Busy/cluttered compositions — the magic should breathe
- Generic sparkle effects — the particles should feel like **ink and light**, not fairy dust
- Hard edges on the split — the two sides blend through the flowing particles, not a sharp line
