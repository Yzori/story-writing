Quiloria — logo concepts

Quill + lore + “-ia”: a place made of stories. Monochrome on purpose — form first, palette later.

A · The Quill Ring

A quill laid across the world it writes. The ring is a hidden Q; the nib slips out as its tail, shedding a single Ink Drop, and a Spark rises where the feather touched. Name, currency and the love-mechanic in one mark.

32 px20 px

B · The Drop of Stories

One Ink Drop, holding an open book in its negative space. The quietest mark of the set — reads at any size, works as an app icon, and literally says “stories come from ink.”

32 px20 px

C · The Weave

Three nibs leaning into the same point — writer, illustrator, worldbuilder — leaving a spark of negative space where they meet. The collaboration thesis as geometry.

32 px20 px

D · Wordmark + lockup

Fraunces, with the dot of the “i” replaced by a falling Ink Drop — the smallest possible intervention that makes the name unmistakably ours. Shown alone and locked up with the Quill Ring.
