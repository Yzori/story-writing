The Door Under Rain

Mira waited until the last lamp in the arcade guttered out. Rain gathered in the glass roof above her, making a trembling ceiling of black water and gold reflections.

The door was not there when she looked directly at it. It preferred the corner of the eye, the half-held breath, the moment when a person was almost brave enough to leave.

In her pocket, the sealed letter warmed like a coal. She had promised not to open it. She had also promised not to come here alone.
